				DOSPlus 5.0 extender version 0.25.16  -  2026-06-15
				================================

Files attached inside this zip:
- DW42-2516.ASM		. . . . . . . . . . .  the source file
- DW42 v0.25.15 SDC-Updater.VDK	 . . .   A virtual file to burn the new version in a SDC bank
- DP50PSR-BCK.ROM	. . . . . . . . . . .   A ROM that can be used in XRoar using Becker Port
- DP50PSR-DW4-2516.ROM	. . . . . . . . . . .   same ROM to be used with a DW4 server (on a disk controller)
- DP50BINS			. . . . . . . . . . .   A binary file that can be loaded from a DW4 server and will start
								  the DP50E v0.25.15 on RAM using Becker Port.
								  switches to MAP1, so for D64 only. Use DLOAD"DP50BINS"
- DP50XINS			. . . . . . . . . . .   A binary file that can be loaded from a DW4 server and will start
								  the DP50E v0.25.15 on RAM for a real DW4 server and D64
								  switches to MAP1, so for D64 only. Use DLOAD"DP50XINS"
- readme.txt			. . . . . . . . . . .   this file
- build.bat the build commands
